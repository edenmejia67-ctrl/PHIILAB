How to read the 3-pin pot (pins 1-2-3)
  • Pin 2 (middle) is the wiper. In this circuit it goes to the + rail with jumper J1.
  • Pin 3 goes to the − rail with J2. Pin 1 is left empty.
  • Which end pin is “3”? Put a meter on Ω between the middle pin and one end pin and turn the knob clockwise. The end pin where the reading falls toward 0 Ω is pin 3 and goes in col 5. The other end pin is pin 1 and goes in col 3.
  • Start with the knob fully counter-clockwise (pin 2↔3 about 10 kΩ). Fully clockwise shorts the supply.
